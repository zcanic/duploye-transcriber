# Lexique 3.83 pronunciations

These files are derived from **Lexique 3.83** by Boris New, Christophe Pallier,
Marc Brysbaert and Ludovic Ferrand (http://www.lexique.org), licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This derived data is
shared under the same licence.

Changes: only the spelling and phonetic columns are kept; homographs keep their most
frequent pronunciation, plus the 3rd-person-plural verb reading when it differs; the
entries are split into one file per three-letter prefix (`tools/build-lexique.mjs`).

> New, B., Pallier, C., Brysbaert, M., & Ferrand, L. (2004). Lexique 2: A new French
> lexical database. *Behavior Research Methods, Instruments, & Computers*, 36(3), 516–524.
