Web component

<duploye-text>

Put Duployé shorthand on any page. Load one script, then wrap French or English in the tag. The shorthand writes itself out when it scrolls into view.

<script type="module" src="https://zcanic.github.io/duploye-transcriber/src/embed.js"></script>

<duploye-text lang="fr" gloss>Un pour tous, tous pour un.</duploye-text>
Un pour tous, tous pour un.

English, in ink blue

<duploye-text lang="en" gloss style="color: #2743b8">Shorthand is the art of writing fast.</duploye-text>
Shorthand is the art of writing fast.

Inline, in a sentence

The French word bonjour is written bonjour in Duployé.

Attributes

AttributeWhat it does
langfr for Duployé (default), en for Sloan-Duployan
glossshow the words under the shorthand
nosiglesspell out abbreviations instead of using sigles or word signs
sizepen size in px per em (default 40)
widthwrap width in px (default: the width of the parent)
staticdraw at once, without the writing animation

The ink takes the element's color; set --duploye-gloss to color the words underneath. Call element.replay() to write it again. Changing the text or an attribute redraws it.
