What to trust

The sounds column follows the rules of the French Duployé method: silent letters and mute e are dropped, [e]/[ɛ] are both é, oi is written a, ui is written i, and elided letters (l', j'…) join the next word only when it is a monosyllable or starts with a circle vowel. The rules are a best guess. Check unusual words.

The drawing comes from the Noto Sans Duployan font, which joins letters and turns vowels using the generic Unicode Duployan model. The French manuals add finer conventions that the font does not always follow: i turns right and é turns left, a vowel goes outside the angle between two consonants, and the nasal vowels use quarter-circle quadrants. Use the output to check how words are broken into sounds and to see the overall shape. For exact calligraphy, follow your manual.
